What ClearWaive does today.

A single reviewed document queue for the compliance paperwork that stalls draws and blows up premium audits. Nothing more than what's built and working.

  • COI expiry alerts. Every certificate tracked to the day, flagged before it lapses.
  • Lien waiver tracking, conditional and unconditional, per pay app, with reminders.
  • Automatic payment holds. Non-compliant subs held from the check run, no manual review.
  • Payment-hold report and CSV export for your bookkeeper

Honest about limits: ClearWaive is a tracking tool, not insurance or legal advice, and does not verify coverage on your behalf. Billing, OCR document scanning, and inbound email parsing are on the roadmap, not shipped yet.
