What it doesn't do

TallyBar is not an analytics suite. No charts, no sparklines, no historical trends beyond a rolling week of local cache. It answers one question — did anything happen since I last looked — and stops there.

It never publishes anything without your explicit confirmation. A review reply shows a confirmation step before it goes live, because there's no undo once it's posted.
