What version 0.1.0 does

Built for people who post to several platforms a week.

Badge counts, aggregated
Unread counts per platform, totalled on the menu bar icon. When a count can't be read, SocialBar says so — it never shows a fake zero.
Compose staging
One hotkey opens every platform's upload flow side by side, with a shared caption to paste.
Separate accounts
Each account gets its own isolated session, kept separate across restarts. The free mode allows one account per platform.
Detached windows
Pop a platform out of the menu bar into its own window.

What it doesn't do

SocialBar stages posts. It does not auto-post. It opens each platform's own upload flow and hands you a shared caption to paste. It does not fill forms, schedule, or upload on your behalf.

That is deliberate. Automated posting breaks whenever a platform changes its interface, and it puts your accounts at risk. Staging just removes the tab-shuffling.
