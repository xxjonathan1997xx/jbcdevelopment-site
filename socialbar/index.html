What it doesn't do

SocialBar stages posts. It does not auto-post. It opens each platform's own upload flow and hands you a shared caption to paste. It does not fill forms, schedule, or upload on your behalf.

That is deliberate. Automated posting breaks whenever a platform changes its interface, and it puts your accounts at risk. Staging just removes the tab-shuffling.
