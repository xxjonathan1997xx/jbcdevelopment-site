Privacy Policy

Privacy for app and software support.

JBC Software Development LLC keeps privacy practices simple: collect only what is needed to respond to support questions, avoid unnecessary sensitive data, and keep app-specific privacy details visible for public releases.

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Information you choose to share

If you contact JBC Software Development LLC by email, you may choose to share your name, email address, app or software details, device details, screenshots, and notes needed to answer your support question.

Do not send passwords, payment information, private customer records, medical information, or other sensitive information through the website or email unless it has been specifically requested through a secure, approved process.

Website contact

This website uses direct email links for support and contact. It does not automatically send forms, store submissions, create leads, or add your information to a database.

The language switcher may save your English or Spanish preference in your browser only. This preference is not sent by this website.

How information is used

Information you choose to send may be used to:

  • Respond to support, privacy, app, or software questions.
  • Understand app, download, setup, or troubleshooting needs.
  • Prepare internal notes after review.
  • Provide support for JBC Software Development LLC apps and software.

Privacy Policy for DraftFlow

Last updated: August 1, 2026

DraftFlow 1.0 processes English dictation with Apple's on-device speech recognition. The shipping app has no DraftFlow account, cloud processing, advertising, analytics, tracking, or synchronized transcript history.

Audio and transcripts

Microphone capture begins only after the user starts dictation. Recordings are temporary and are deleted after successful processing, cancellation, recognition failure, insertion failure, or recorder relaunch. DraftFlow does not collect recordings or transcripts and does not persist transcript history. The latest transcript may remain temporarily in app memory so the user can recover or copy it.

Information stored on the Mac

Personal dictionary entries and app preferences are stored locally until the user edits them, deletes them, or removes the app's local data. DraftFlow does not sell this information or use it for advertising or model training.

Permissions

Microphone permission is used to record only when dictation is started. Speech Recognition permission converts the temporary recording to text on device. Accessibility permission on macOS inserts the result into a focused, non-secure text field; DraftFlow refuses password and secure fields.

Sharing and support

Copying text or sending a support email is an explicit user action. The chosen destination handles that information under its own privacy practices. Users should not send private recordings, passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive dictated content in support requests.

For privacy questions, email support@jbcdevelopment.dev.

Privacy Policy for ThreadVigil

Last updated: July 30, 2026

ThreadVigil reads supported local coding-agent session metadata and bounded transcript portions needed to determine status or generate a handoff requested by the user. Processing occurs on the Mac.

ThreadVigil stores normalized session metadata for seven days rather than full transcripts. It does not access OpenCode credential or account tables, sell session information, use it for advertising or model training, or operate a cloud account service.

Routine network access is limited to signed update checks through Sparkle. Users can remove ThreadVigil’s local Application Support data at any time.

For privacy questions, email support@jbcdevelopment.dev.

Privacy Policy for NextRole

Last updated: July 15, 2026

NextRole is operated by JBC Software Development LLC and provides tools for organizing a job search. A user may explore synthetic sample data without an account. Signed-in users use passwordless email authentication so their information can be stored in an owner-scoped account and accessed across supported devices.

Information users provide

Information a user chooses to enter can include an email address, display name, phone number, free-form location, structured resume content, career profile, role and work preferences, desired salary, saved-job salary ranges and posting links, application status, dates, and notes. NextRole assigns an account identifier to keep each user's records separate.

NextRole does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, inbox, calendar, or precise device location. The initial public beta does not accept uploaded resume files.

How information is used

NextRole uses information to authenticate and maintain an account; provide resume, onboarding, Today, Jobs, Tracker, and Strategy features; save and synchronize records a user enters; prepare account exports; process account deletion; protect and operate the service; investigate failures; prevent abuse; and respond to support requests.

NextRole does not use advertising, behavioral tracking, third-party product analytics, or external AI. It does not automatically apply to jobs or monitor email or calendars. NextRole does not sell personal information.

Service providers and operational data

NextRole uses Supabase for passwordless authentication, owner-scoped database storage, account export, account deletion, security, debugging, and service availability. Supabase and its network provider can process and temporarily retain authentication and request metadata such as user ID, IP address, country derived from IP address, user-agent, request path, response status, and latency. The current Supabase Pro configuration retains API/database logs for seven days and daily database backups for seven days. NextRole does not configure a separate log drain or long-term analytics copy.

NextRole uses Resend to deliver magic-link sign-in email. Resend processes the recipient email address, sender identity, message content, delivery status, and related delivery or security metadata. Resend documents 30-day retention for email data. Resume, saved-job, application, and strategy content is not sent through Resend.

JBC Software Development LLC processes only information a user voluntarily sends to support@jbcdevelopment.dev for support, privacy, deletion, or security assistance. Users should not email passwords, authentication links, tokens, full account exports, or unnecessary resume content.

Job-posting links and exports

User-entered job-posting links open external websites only when the user chooses to visit them. Those websites operate under their own privacy practices.

Resume PDF export and account JSON export occur only when initiated by the user. The user chooses where an exported file is saved or shared, and that destination handles the exported copy under its own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Account and job-search records remain while an account is active unless the user edits or deletes them. Signed-in users can request a complete JSON export of active account data and can permanently delete the account from Account settings. Account deletion immediately removes the active authentication account and owner-scoped database records. Previously created encrypted backups can retain the deleted state until the seven-day Supabase backup window expires. NextRole does not use backups to recreate an individually deleted account. Files a user previously exported or shared must be deleted separately from the locations the user chose.

Security

NextRole uses encrypted network connections, owner-scoped database policies, authenticated sessions, restricted administrative credentials, and release checks intended to prevent one account from accessing another account's records. No system can guarantee absolute security. A user who believes an account or information is at risk should contact support promptly.

Children

NextRole is a general job-search organization service and is not directed to children under 13. If JBC Software Development LLC learns that information was collected from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, it will take steps to delete it.

Changes

This policy may be updated when NextRole's features, providers, or legal obligations change. The last-updated date will be revised, and material changes will be communicated through an appropriate service or support channel.

Contact

For support, privacy questions, export help, deletion problems, or security concerns, email support@jbcdevelopment.dev.

Privacy Policy for PainTrail

Last updated: July 31, 2026

PainTrail is a personal record-keeping app designed so that health information stays under the user's control. PainTrail stores pain check-ins, medication records, visit-preparation notes and questions, app settings, and Pro unlock status locally on the device. PainTrail does not operate a server and does not automatically upload this information.

PainTrail provides a manual JSON backup. The backup contains pain check-ins and medication records only. It does not include visit details, questions, the pain profile, app settings, or Pro purchase state. The user chooses where to save or share the file through the iOS share sheet. PainTrail does not automatically upload backups to iCloud or any other service. Once a file leaves PainTrail, the selected destination handles that copy under its own terms and privacy practices.

PainTrail does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking software; create a user account; or sell or rent user data. Information leaves local app storage only when the user initiates PDF sharing or a manual JSON export.

PainTrail offers an optional one-time Pro purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. JBC Software Development LLC does not receive or store payment information.

If a user voluntarily participates in the PainTrail First 100 feedback campaign, the user may email JBC Software Development LLC an eligibility confirmation and honest product feedback. The email address, received timestamp, feedback, eligibility confirmation, and reward-fulfillment status are used only to improve PainTrail, administer the campaign, prevent duplicate rewards, and deliver an Apple offer code. Participants should not send pain entries, screenshots, diagnoses, medications, or other health information. An App Store rating or review is never required. Campaign records are retained only as reasonably needed for product feedback, campaign administration, fraud prevention, and legal or accounting obligations.

Deleting PainTrail removes data stored inside the app from the device. PainTrail does not currently provide an in-app reset, delete-all, or individual-entry deletion control. PDF and JSON files previously shared or exported must be deleted separately from the locations where the user chose to save them.

PainTrail is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, medication guidance, or emergency support. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical decisions.

Privacy Policy for Wave Rush!

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Wave Rush! is an offline, single-player jet-ski racing game published by JBC Software Development LLC. The game runs entirely on the device and does not operate a server, use analytics, use advertising, or include third-party tracking.

Information stored on the device

Wave Rush! saves a small amount of data locally on the device only, using the operating system's standard local storage. This never leaves the device and is never transmitted to JBC Software Development LLC: best scores for each track, and an audio/rhythm calibration setting. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Information not collected

Wave Rush! does not collect and does not transmit a user's name, email, address, phone number, or any contact information; location data; device identifiers used for tracking or advertising; analytics or usage telemetry; or photos, contacts, microphone, or camera data. The app requests no such permissions and contains no networking code for these purposes.

In-app purchases

Wave Rush! may offer an optional in-app purchase to unlock additional content. When a purchase is made, it is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. JBC Software Development LLC does not receive, see, or store payment card details, billing address, or other financial information.

Game Center

Wave Rush! may use Apple's Game Center to show leaderboards. When a user chooses to use Game Center, participation is governed by Apple's Game Center service, not by JBC Software Development LLC. Game Center may associate scores with a user's Game Center nickname and share them with other players on the leaderboards. JBC Software Development LLC does not operate the leaderboard servers and does not receive Apple account information.

Children

Wave Rush! does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it does not create the risks that laws such as COPPA and GDPR-K are designed to address.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Wave Rush!'s features change — for example, if a future version adds in-app purchases or Game Center. The last-updated date above will be revised when it does.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Wave Rush!'s privacy practices, email support@jbcdevelopment.dev.
