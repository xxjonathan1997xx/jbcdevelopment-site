Privacy Policy

Privacy for website inquiries and app support.

JBC Software Development LLC keeps privacy practices simple: collect only what is needed to respond to inquiries, avoid unnecessary sensitive data, and review app-specific privacy needs before launch.

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Information you choose to share

If you contact JBC Software Development LLC by email or prepare an inquiry through the website form, you may choose to share your name, business name, email address, phone number, project needs, timeline, budget range, and notes.

Do not send passwords, payment information, private customer records, medical information, or other sensitive information through the website or email unless it has been specifically requested through a secure, approved process.

Website intake form

The website intake form is currently static. It validates the required fields in your browser, prepares a copyable inquiry summary, and creates an email draft for you to review before sending.

The form does not automatically send email, store submissions, create leads, or add your information to a database.

The language switcher may save your English or Spanish preference in your browser only. This preference is not sent by this website.

How information is used

Information you choose to send may be used to:

  • Respond to business, project, support, or app-related inquiries.
  • Understand requested services or software needs.
  • Prepare internal notes, estimates, or proposal drafts after review.
  • Provide support for JBC Software Development LLC products or services.

App privacy policies

App-specific privacy details may vary by product. When a JBC Software Development LLC app is released publicly, its App Store listing or support materials should identify any app-specific privacy practices that apply.

Third-party services

JBC Software Development LLC may use ordinary business services such as email, hosting, domain services, analytics, app stores, development tools, or support tools. These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Data retention

Inquiry and support information may be kept as long as needed to respond, maintain records, manage projects, or meet normal business needs. You may ask for correction or deletion of information you previously provided.
