Assetto Corsa track

Miami Marine Stadium AutoX

  • Version 0.1 (test release)
  • 53 MB zip
  • No Custom Shaders Patch needed

A fan-made autocross on the Miami Marine Stadium lot on Virginia Key, inspired by the July 26, 2026 AutoCross event. A 918 m timed run with a start and finish, cones, staging, spectators and the stadium on the water.

Download (53 MB) More screenshots

First test release, so feedback is welcome. Fan-made and not affiliated with Kunos Simulazioni, the event organizers, or the Miami Marine Stadium. The course follows a concept layout, not the official cone layout.

Install

  1. Content Manager: drag the zip onto the Content Manager window and click Install.
  2. By hand: open the zip and copy the content folder into your Assetto Corsa folder (usually steamapps\common\assettocorsa).
  3. Start a Practice session on Miami Marine Stadium AutoX.

How to drive it

  • Your run time is the Sector 1 split, from START to FINISH.
  • After the finish, drive back across the lot through the START arch for the next run.
  • Cones are drive-through; count your own hits (+2 seconds each).
