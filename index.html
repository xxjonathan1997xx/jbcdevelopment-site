SocialBar puts 11 social platforms in your Mac's menu bar.

Unread counts for Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, YouTube Studio, Bluesky, Pinterest and Reddit, one click away. Write a caption once, adjust it per platform, and open every upload flow side by side.

v0.2.0macOS 14+Signed and notarizedFree

DOES

  • Shows unread counts for 11 platforms in the menu bar
  • One caption, adjusted per platform, with character limits
  • Opens every upload flow side by side with one hotkey
  • Keeps separate sessions for multiple accounts

DOESN'T

  • Auto-post, schedule or upload for you, on purpose
  • Fill in forms on the platforms
  • Need an account
  • Send analytics or telemetry from the app

ClearWaive holds any payment that isn't backed by current paperwork.

For small general contractors. It tracks each subcontractor's certificate of insurance, lien waivers and expiry dates, and keeps non-compliant subs out of the check run.

Private betaFree during betaWeb

DOES

  • Tracks COIs and lien waivers per vendor, per pay app
  • Flags certificates before they lapse
  • Holds non-compliant subs from the check run automatically
  • Exports the payment-hold list as CSV

DOESN'T

  • Give insurance or legal advice
  • Verify coverage on your behalf
  • Handle billing, document scanning or email parsing yet

CounterOps runs a parts counter, in English and Spanish.

Built for independent parts distributors: search by part number or barcode, sell from one screen, work the exceptions queue, and give the owner a private view of sales and approvals on any phone.

Active pilotBilingual EN/ESDesktop and phone

DOES

  • Finds parts by SKU, barcode, manufacturer number or cross-reference
  • Puts stock, bin, price, core and cart in one split view
  • Queues receiving discrepancies, low stock and transfers by priority
  • Gives the owner a private view on desktop or phone

DOESN'T, YET

  • Run production accounting
  • Process payments
  • Run on live data: the pilot uses realistic sample data until migration is signed off
