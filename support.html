Support

Support for apps, software projects, and business inquiries.

Use this page for app support, website questions, project inquiries, privacy questions, or general contact with JBC Software Development LLC.

Contact support

Email jonathan@jbcdevelopment.dev for support, business, app, website, AI tool, or automation questions.

What to include

For the fastest review, include:

  • Your name and the app, website, or project you are asking about.
  • A short description of the issue or request.
  • The device, browser, or operating system if this is a technical issue.
  • Steps to reproduce a bug, if relevant.
  • Any screenshot or error message that helps explain the issue.

App support

For current or future JBC Software Development LLC apps, include the app name and the version number if available. App-specific support links may be added as each product is released.

Business project inquiries

For small business software, AI tools, dashboards, automation, or app MVPs, use the intake form on the home page or send a short email explaining the workflow problem you need help solving.

Safety and privacy

Do not send passwords, payment details, private customer records, or other sensitive data by email unless a secure process has been approved first.

Response expectations

Support and business inquiries are reviewed manually. No automatic support tickets, proposals, billing, deployments, or external actions are created from this website.
