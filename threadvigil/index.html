Local by design.

ThreadVigil reads supported local coding-agent session data to determine status, estimate costs, and generate a handoff only when requested. It stores normalized metadata for seven days rather than full transcripts. Routine network access is limited to Sparkle update checks.

Cursor detail depends on the stable, privacy-safe local formats available on your Mac and may be limited to process-level monitoring.
