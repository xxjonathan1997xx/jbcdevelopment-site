PainTrail First 100

Become one of PainTrail’s First 100. Get Premium for life.

Use PainTrail, record your first pain entry, and submit honest product feedback. The first 100 verified participants receive lifetime Premium access.

Official terms last updated: July 31, 2026

How to claim

  1. Install PainTrail and record your first pain entry. Your health information stays on your device.
  2. Use the email button below to confirm completion and submit honest feedback about the product experience.
  3. Do not include pain-entry contents, screenshots, diagnoses, medications, or other health information.
  4. If your complete submission is among the first 100 valid entries, we reply with a one-time Apple redemption code.

Prepare feedback email

Before sending: replace the bracketed prompt with your product feedback. No health details, pain-entry contents, screenshots, App Store rating, or App Store review are needed.

Official offer terms

  • Open to people age 18 or older where PainTrail and its Apple in-app purchases are available, except where prohibited.
  • A qualifying submission requires the participant to install PainTrail, record a first pain entry, and send the fixed eligibility confirmation with meaningful, honest product feedback.
  • The first 100 valid submissions are determined by the timestamp at which the complete feedback email reaches jbcsdevs@gmail.com.
  • Feedback may be positive, neutral, or critical. No App Store rating or review is required, and the content of any rating or review does not affect eligibility.
  • Each person may receive one reward. Duplicate or automated submissions, false confirmations, placeholder feedback, and attempts to manipulate the offer are ineligible.
  • The reward is one Apple offer code granting PainTrail Premium through the app’s non-consumable Premium product. There is no cash alternative or transfer.
  • “For life” means the Premium entitlement does not have a recurring expiration while PainTrail remains available and supported. It does not guarantee that the app or App Store will operate indefinitely.
  • Winners must redeem their code within 30 days of delivery and before Apple’s stated code expiration date.
  • Participants must not be asked to post their feedback publicly. Ratings and reviews are never required or rewarded.
  • JBC Software Development LLC may reject fraudulent or incomplete claims and may modify, suspend, or end the offer if fraud, technical failure, legal requirements, or Apple platform limitations prevent fair operation.
  • This offer is sponsored solely by JBC Software Development LLC. Apple is not a sponsor and is not involved in administering the offer.

Privacy

Feedback emails are used only to improve PainTrail, verify eligibility, prevent duplicate rewards, deliver the Apple code, and administer this campaign. Do not send health information. Campaign records are retained only as reasonably needed for product feedback, administration, fraud prevention, and legal or accounting obligations. Read the PainTrail privacy policy.

The offer begins when JBC Software Development LLC publishes its first public campaign announcement and ends when 100 valid rewards have been claimed or the offer is otherwise ended under these terms.
