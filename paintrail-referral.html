PainTrail First 100

Refer a friend. Get PainTrail Premium for life.

When your friend installs PainTrail and records their first pain entry, you’ll receive lifetime Premium access. Limited to the first 100 verified referrals.

Official terms last updated: July 31, 2026

How to claim

  1. Share PainTrail with a friend and tell them you may receive free Premium if they qualify.
  2. Your friend installs PainTrail and records their first pain entry. Their health information stays on their device.
  3. Your friend prepares the confirmation email below and copies you, the referrer. They must not include pain details or screenshots.
  4. If the claim is among the first 100 valid referrals, JBC Development replies to the referrer with a one-time Apple redemption code.

Prepare confirmation email

Before sending: add the person who referred you to the Cc field. No health details, pain-entry contents, or screenshots are needed.

Official offer terms

  • Open to people age 18 or older where PainTrail and its Apple in-app purchases are available, except where prohibited.
  • A qualifying referral requires a genuinely new PainTrail user to install the app, record a first pain entry, and send the confirmation email while copying the referrer.
  • The first 100 valid claims are determined by the timestamp at which the complete confirmation reaches jbcsdevs@gmail.com.
  • Each person may receive one reward. Self-referrals, duplicate or automated claims, false confirmations, and attempts to manipulate the offer are ineligible.
  • The reward is one Apple offer code granting PainTrail Premium through the app’s non-consumable Premium product. There is no cash alternative or transfer.
  • “For life” means the Premium entitlement does not have a recurring expiration while PainTrail remains available and supported. It does not guarantee that the app or App Store will operate indefinitely.
  • Winners must redeem their code within 30 days of delivery and before Apple’s stated code expiration date.
  • Public referral posts must clearly disclose the incentive, for example: “I may receive free PainTrail Premium if you join through my referral.” Ratings and reviews are never required.
  • JBC Software Development LLC may reject fraudulent or incomplete claims and may modify, suspend, or end the offer if fraud, technical failure, legal requirements, or Apple platform limitations prevent fair operation.
  • This offer is sponsored solely by JBC Software Development LLC. Apple is not a sponsor and is not involved in administering the offer.

Referral privacy

Referral emails are used only to verify eligibility, prevent duplicate rewards, deliver the Apple code, and administer this campaign. Do not send health information. Campaign records are retained only as reasonably needed for administration, fraud prevention, and legal or accounting obligations. Read the PainTrail privacy policy.

The offer begins when JBC Software Development LLC publishes its first public campaign announcement and ends when 100 valid rewards have been claimed or the offer is otherwise ended under these terms.
