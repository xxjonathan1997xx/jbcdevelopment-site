# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

iOS app users, Mac tool power users, small general contractors (ClearWaive), and potential client partners looking for custom mobile apps or Mac automation tools built by an independent Florida developer.

## Product Purpose

`jbcdevelopment.dev` is the official company website and product catalog for JBC Software Development LLC. It showcases active iOS apps and Mac tools, provides privacy and support resources, and offers scoped custom software development services.

## Positioning

Indie software engineering studio built on privacy, transparency, and signed releases. Ships native Apple software (iOS apps, notarized macOS tools) with zero tracking, clear support paths, and verified checksums.

## Operating Context

- Independent studio operating out of Florida
- Direct email contact: `jbcsdevs@gmail.com`
- Primary products: Paw Care Academy, PainTrail, ClearWaive, M5SteamBridge, SocialBar
- Static site deployment (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages)
- Lightweight English / Spanish (EN/ES) language switcher in browser storage

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No server-side form handlers — contact links launch mailto drafts
- Static HTML/CSS/JS architecture with no build step
- Fast load times and accessible semantic markup
- App Store links, direct notarized DMG download links, and checksum validation

## Brand Commitments

- Name: JBC Software Development LLC (JBC Development)
- Brand palette: Deep electric blue (`#0b5fff`), sky blue (`#38bdf8`), dark navy slate background (`#05101d`), clean white surfaces
- Privacy-first commitment: No ads, no third-party trackers, no hidden data collection

## Product Principles

1. **Craft over clutter**: Let the software speak through clean visual hierarchy and direct proof, not wall-to-wall badges.
2. **Privacy as a feature**: Highlight transparent data practices clearly without overwhelming the visitor.
3. **Actionable navigation**: Make it effortless to explore apps, download utilities, or reach out for custom work.
